3. SECURE PATIENT HEALTH DATA EXCHANGE
 
This project utilizes a decentralized architecture to allow for the
secure sharing of medical records between different hospitals and
diagnostic labs. It empowers patients with ownership of their
medical history and provides doctors with a comprehensive, verified
view of a patient’s health, regardless of where they were previously
treated.
